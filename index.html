Wholesale Information

Buying from Jolijou

01
Order from One Item
There is no minimum quantity or spend. Catalog prices are wholesale rates.
02
Payment & Export Discounts
Pay in full before dispatch. Export orders paid via Wise receive 3% off below JPY 1,000,000, or 5% off from JPY 1,000,000 per transaction.
03
Dispatch & Delivery
Usually dispatched 1–5 business days after payment confirmation, subject to availability. We use FedEx, DHL and EMS; contact us about urgent pickup.
04
Shipping Fees & Import Charges
Shipping is calculated at checkout. Import duties and taxes are generally payable by the buyer. DDP quotes are available for selected regions.
05
Condition & Returns
Sales are generally final. For significant undisclosed damage or authenticity issues, contact us; eligible items are refunded after return. Report defects within 7 days of receipt.
06
Questions Before Ordering?
Ask about an item, its condition or delivery options. Read our FAQ or contact our Tokyo team.
